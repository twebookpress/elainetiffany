The series

Fate dealt the cards.
The cats chose the women.

When Alessandra's familiar, Hazel, gives birth to seven magical black kittens, each kitten sets out to find a young woman destined to become a conjurer. Their new ladies receive gifts of luck and love—but those gifts come with a lesson. Before their magic can reach its full power, each woman must learn that love is the strongest magic of all.

From New Orleans to Marfa, Pensacola, Spokane and beyond, every novella brings a new kitten, a new tarot card, a new setting, and a sweet contemporary romance touched by magic.
